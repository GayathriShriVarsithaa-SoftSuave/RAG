TITLE: Household Policy Wording - Exclusions Schedule (2023 edition)

## Scope

This schedule applies to household buildings and contents cover. It lists the causes of loss that are excluded. An adjuster decides whether an exclusion applies to a given loss.

## Exclusions Table

| Code | Description | Applies To (Excluded?) |
| ---- | ----------- | ---------------------- |
| EXC-01 | Flood: loss or damage caused by the overflow of a river, stream or the sea. Water that runs off the surface of land after heavy rain, and groundwater seepage, are not excluded by this clause. | Yes - excluded |
| EXC-02 | Wear and tear: gradual deterioration, or old and worn-out materials. | Yes - excluded |
| EXC-03 | Intentional damage caused deliberately by the policyholder. | Yes - excluded |
| EXC-04 | Vacant property: loss at a property left unoccupied for more than 60 days. | Yes - excluded |
| EXC-05 | Business use: business stock or loss arising from business activity at the property. | Yes - excluded |
