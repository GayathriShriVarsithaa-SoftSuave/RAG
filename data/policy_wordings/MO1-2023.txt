TITLE: Motor Policy Wording - Exclusions Schedule (2023 edition)

## Scope

This schedule applies to private motor cover. It lists the causes of loss that are excluded. An adjuster decides whether an exclusion applies to a given loss.

## Exclusions Table

| Code | Description | Applies To (Excluded?) |
| ---- | ----------- | ---------------------- |
| EXC-01 | Water damage to the engine caused by driving into standing water or flood water. | Yes - excluded |
| EXC-02 | Wear and tear: mechanical breakdown or gradual deterioration of parts. | Yes - excluded |
| EXC-03 | Racing, rallying or any competitive use of the vehicle. | Yes - excluded |
